About DevJams

DevJams is more than just a hackathon—it's a movement to bring together the brightest minds in VIT to solve real-world problems and push the boundaries of innovation.

Since the start of our club, we've hosted over 20 hackathons in VIT, bringing together more than 10,000 students from diverse backgrounds. Our events have resulted in groundbreaking projects, successful startups, and countless friendships.

Our Mission

To create an inclusive environment where developers, designers, and innovators can collaborate, learn, and build solutions that make a real impact on the world.

What Makes Us Different

  • Built by students, for students
  • Hands-on problem statements focused on learning, creativity, and impact
  • Beginner-friendly & inclusive — all skill levels welcome
  • Fast-paced, high-energy hackathon with real deliverables
  • Cool prizes, swag, and bragging rights 🏆
  • A chance to build, ship, and showcase your ideas in 48 hours

Join Our Community

Whether you're a seasoned developer or just starting your coding journey, DevJams welcomes you. Connect with like-minded individuals, learn new skills, and be part of something bigger.
